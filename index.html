天の音、人と未来をひとつにA Heavenly Sound, Uniting People and the Future

すべては、一つの響きから始まる。Everything begins with a single sound.

世界は響きで満ちている。The world is filled with sound.

風が木々を揺らす音。The sound of wind moving through trees.

雨が大地を潤す音。The sound of rain nourishing the earth.

鼓動が命を刻む音。The sound of a heartbeat marking life.

私たちは、その一音に想いを込める。Into each single sound, we pour our heart.

一音一音を大切に。Cherishing every single note.

一歩一歩を大切に。Cherishing every single step.

一人一人を大切に。Cherishing every single person.

その響きが、人と人を結び、May these sounds bring people together,

心に残る一瞬となることを願って。and become a moment that lingers in the heart.

風のように自由に。響きのように永く。Free as the wind. Lasting as the sound.

私たちは、音を届けるのではない。We do not simply deliver sound.

人の心に、余韻を届ける。We deliver a lingering resonance to the heart.

ABOUT

天音座についてABOUT AMANEZA

PURPOSE

活動目的Purpose

和太鼓を中心に、篠笛・箏・尺八その他の和楽器を融合した演奏活動を通じ、日本文化の継承及び発展に寄与するとともに、地域社会との交流及び文化振興に貢献することを目的とする。Through performances featuring Japanese taiko drums and other traditional instruments, including the shinobue, koto, and shakuhachi, we aim to preserve and further develop Japanese culture while fostering community engagement and promoting cultural enrichment.

POLICY

活動方針Policy

礼節・協調性・向上心を大切にし、会員相互が互いを尊重しながら、演奏技術と人間性の向上を目指す。We value courtesy, cooperation, and a spirit of self-improvement, growing as performers and as individuals while respecting one another as members.

ACTIVITIES

主な活動Activities

  • 演奏会及び地域イベントへの出演Performances at concerts and local community events
  • 企業・商業施設等のイベントへの出演Performances at corporate and commercial facility events
  • 福祉施設及び教育機関での演奏活動Performances at welfare facilities and educational institutions
  • 定期練習及び和楽器の研鑽Regular practice and refinement of traditional instrument skills
  • 日本文化の普及及び継承活動Activities to promote and pass on Japanese culture

GREETING

座長挨拶Greeting

太鼓和奏集団 天音座の座長を務めております、柴田 康弘と申します。My name is Yasuhiro Shibata, and I serve as chairman of AMANEZA Japanese Taiko and Traditional Instrument Ensemble.

2026年6月、和太鼓を中心に篠笛・箏・尺八などの和楽器を通じて、日本文化の継承と地域社会への貢献を志す仲間たちと、この天音座を立ち上げました。In June 2026, I founded AMANEZA together with fellow members who share a passion for preserving Japanese culture and contributing to the local community through taiko and traditional instruments such as shinobue, koto, and shakuhachi.

一音一音に想いを込め、一人一人を大切にしながら、聴いてくださる方の心に静かな余韻を届けられるよう、これからも真摯に稽古を重ねてまいります。Putting our hearts into every single sound and valuing each member as an individual, we will keep training sincerely so that we may leave a quiet, lasting impression in the hearts of those who listen.

発足したばかりの小さな一座ではございますが、皆様に見守っていただきながら、一歩一歩、着実に歩んでまいりたいと存じます。今後ともどうぞよろしくお願いいたします。We are still a small, newly formed group, but with your support and guidance, we hope to move forward steadily, one step at a time. We appreciate your continued support.

座長　柴田 康弘Chairman, Yasuhiro Shibata
